# Chapter One — Lumen's Beacon

The existing Emerald opening remains the prologue. Choose the middle starter
(Pikachu), finish Birch's rescue, then talk to **Elara**, the scientist at
Littleroot's central path. Her beacon leads to **Lumen Crossing**. Existing saves
can use the same entrance; no new game is intentionally required.

Lumen Crossing has a lodge with free healing, an evolution workshop, a quest
researcher, habitat hints, and a beacon Gym. Doors link to actual interiors.
Return to Littleroot through the southern beacon; the northern gate enters
Riftway Trail. Ordinary Hoenn progression remains accessible.

1. Ask the Lumen researcher or Oldale researcher to begin the anomaly quest.
2. Investigate the researchers on Routes 101, 102 and 103, in order.
3. Return to Lumen. Follow Riftway Trail and its eastern breach into Rift Hollow.
4. Defeat Vesper and claim the Shiny Charm. A full Bag does not forfeit it.
5. Return to Lumen and challenge Astra in the beacon Gym for the Rift Badge.
   This uses the first badge's existing save flags, trainer flags and progression
   variables; it is not a ninth independent badge. The Rustboro counterpart
   shares the result, preventing duplicate progression.
6. Battle rival Nova on Riftway Trail. The lodge healer then awards a Light Ball.
7. With the Dynamo Badge, enter Stormscar through Riftway's northern gate.
   Defeat Vesper and meet Nova before challenging the guardian Zapdos.
   Fainting it or retreating does not permanently remove the encounter; catching
   it marks the quest complete and removes the guardian.

The researcher reports the next unfinished objective. The collector describes
Riftway habitats. The workshop sells evolution stones, Linking Cords and rare
items, including modern evolution materials. Ordinary field supplies still
arrive when opening the Bag, without duplicate entries.

The chapter includes authored outdoor layouts, reused native building/interior
art, an original rival and Eclipse scout, two Rift locations, and the first
badge encounter. It is an opening chapter layered into Hoenn, not a completed
replacement for the entire Emerald campaign. The original title-screen artwork,
further story chapters, classic-region travel and a complete availability audit
remain separate milestones. A full chapter playthrough in Delta is still needed.
