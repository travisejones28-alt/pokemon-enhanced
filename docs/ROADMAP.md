# Shattered Realms roadmap

## Implemented in the current source

- Pikachu starter, eight signatures and friendship/Bond attacks.
- Full current/pre-evolution teachable/egg/level libraries, forward/backward pages,
  a persistent exceptional-attack journal and old-tail migration.
- Player unlimited PP, money and specified consumables; reusable TMs; 4x battle EXP.
- Adaptive trainers, corrected IV floors, smarter switching and explicit competitive
  sets for the eight first Gym fights, Elite Four, Champion and Vesper.
- Enemy extended attacks across turns in ordinary single trainer fights:
  level-legal level-up library rotates into the fourth active slot with PP
  bookkeeping, Hard/Extreme gating, and special-mode/forced-move safeguards.
- In-game Shattered Rules page with persistent save-variable configuration.
- Native followers, Pikachu interaction messages and retained Bond unlocks.
- Lumen Crossing with lodge, workshop and Gym; Riftway Trail; Stormscar sanctuary;
  original rival/scout; anomaly quest, first boss, first badge and guardian quest.
- Expanded encounter tables and habitat hints, evolution workshop and local
  alternatives for every trade-evolution target in the current species sources.
- Stable main save/Pokémon layouts, appended map IDs, reserved-tail migration,
  host tests, ARM footprint checks and targeted mGBA regression tests.

## Acceptance still required

- Full chapter playthrough and real old-save round trip in Delta.
- All battle-page/effect combinations, especially Choice locks, multi-turn moves,
  target selection and double-battle cancellation.
- Follower visuals on every transition and travel mode.
- End-to-end Gym/rival/Elite Four/Champion balance with 4x EXP and Rare Candies.
- New title art and a fully original opening replacing the retained prologue.

## Later campaign

- Further connected Rift Realms and antagonist/rival arcs.
- Classic-region travel with proper engine/script compatibility work.
- More legendary/mythical quests and optional exploration rewards.
- Full species availability/evolution audit and intentional distribution.
- Broader collection tracking and quality-of-life refinements.
- A larger history format only if hardware/save capacity can support it safely;
  never silently discard old exceptional attacks to make room.

The first original chapter is layered into Hoenn so old saves and existing
progression remain usable. It is not a claim that the entire game is finished.
